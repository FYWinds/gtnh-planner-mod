## New

- **Simplified Chinese.** The whole planner reads in Chinese when the game does. Machine and material names are the ones the GTNH Chinese pack uses.
- **Wires share a trunk.** When one output feeds several cards its wires leave as one line and fork near the cards. Settings has a switch for it.
- **Wires into one input share a trunk too.** When several cards feed one input their wires join near those cards and come in as one line. The same switch turns it off.
- The Extreme Industrial Greenhouse, the Integrated Ore Factory and the Large Essentia Smeltery are among the non-recipe machines, under Other. Pick the seed, or the ore and its mode, or wire in what to smelt, and the card shows what the machine makes and uses, worked out the way the machine does it. Essentia shows on the card but cannot be wired.
- TwistSpaceTechnology machines work out their own speed, power and parallels on cards.
- TwistSpaceTechnology's Universal Generator is among the non-recipe machines, with the Engines: pick its fuel, dynamo tier and dynamo amps, and it burns gas or diesel fuels the way the machine does.
- A fuel recipe from NEI put on a multiblock generator that is also a non-recipe machine (a Solid-Oxide Fuel Cell, the Large Gas Turbine, the Large Semifluid Generator, a TwistSpaceTechnology generator and the like) becomes that generator's non-recipe card burning the fuel, with its EU, instead of a card with no power out or one that runs as another machine. Switching a card's machine to one of them does the same and keeps its wires.
- TwistSpaceTechnology's Tower of Abstraction (with Solar: pick its pieces and eggs) and Mega Naquadah Reactor (with Endgame: pick its fuel, coolant, booster, parallels and running time) are among the non-recipe machines, and make the power the machines make.
- TwistSpaceTechnology's Basic Steam Generator (with the Turbines), Large Solar Boiler (with the Boilers) and Lightning Spire (with the Engines) are among the non-recipe machines too, each with the settings its output depends on: dynamos, the boiler's heat and calcification, the spire's lightning rods.
- TwistSpaceTechnology's Dyson Sphere Ray Receiving Station and Artificial Star are among the non-recipe machines, with the Endgame ones: set the sphere, planet and controller slot, or the fuels and field blocks, and they make the wireless EU (or Critical Photons) the machines do.
- The Void Miners I to III, the Planetary Gas Siphon and the Space Pumping Modules Mk-I to Mk-III are among the non-recipe machines, under Other: pick the dimension and noble gas, the planet, depth, energy hatch and coils, or each module's recipes and parallels, and the card shows every ore or fluid at the game's rate, read from the game's own ore tables and pumping recipes.
- More TwistSpaceTechnology machines on cards: the Miracle Door, the Indistinct Tentacle Prototype MK-II, the Hyper Thermal Convector, the Large Steam Forge Hammer and Large Steam Alloy Smelter (they burn steam), and the Swelegfyr Blast Furnace (its heat is a setting, it burns Blazing Pyrotheum, and its glass caps the energy hatch tier). The Silksong, Indistinct Tentacle, Thermal Energy Devourer and Deployed Nano Core have a Power Source setting for their wireless mode, which runs fixed batches on wireless network EU. The Miracle Door's Critical Photons are an input once you set how many crafts a run, and its White Dwarf Mold setting casts molten outputs into ingots.
- More TwistSpaceTechnology 0.8.1 machines on cards: the DTPF Prototype MK-II and Large Neutron Oscillator with their execution cores, the Industrial Magic Matrix and Alchemy Tower, the Mega Digester, the TST Processing Array and Big Bro Array, Ball Lightning's fusion mode, the Intensify Chemical Distorter's chemical plant mode, Hephaestus' Atelier's furnace mode and higher coils, and Biosphere III's bacterial vat. Every GregTech cutter's NEI recipes now open on a card.
- TwistSpaceTechnology machines with their own NEI pages work on cards: Nether Interface, Super Water Purifier, General Ore Processing Factory, Extreme Crafting Center, Skypiercer Tower, Strange Matter Aggregator, Dyson Sphere Module Launch Site, Bottled Chaos and Eco-Sphere Simulator, each with the settings its run depends on.

## Better

- Long Chinese text breaks onto new lines instead of running off the edge.

## Fixed

- Generators and other power sources can be added to a plan again while you are signed in. They used to vanish at once with "An edit could not be saved", and edits to them were undone.
- Recipes with an empty slot, like the Bricked Blast Furnace's, no longer crash the game when you hover their add to plan button or bring them in from a plan.
- Fewer crashes on the board: hovering a card or port just as it changed, a machine count tip on a card that needs almost no machines, and going back to the planner from NEI's recipe page.
- The library no longer crashes on some plan icons.
- Clay Blocks, raw meat and the other things GregTech's recycling recipes take now wire into those recipes.
- The numpad types into rate and count boxes. An IME types Chinese into notes, task text and the other text boxes.
- Steam multiblocks and structure pictures show properly when the game is not in English.
