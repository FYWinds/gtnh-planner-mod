## New

- **Simplified Chinese.** The whole planner reads in Chinese when the game does. Machine and material names are the ones the GTNH Chinese pack uses.
- **Wires share a trunk.** When one output feeds several cards its wires leave as one line and fork near the cards. Settings has a switch for it.
- TwistSpaceTechnology machines work out their own speed, power and parallels on cards.
- TwistSpaceTechnology's Universal Generator is among the non-recipe machines, with the Engines: pick its fuel, dynamo tier and dynamo amps, and it burns gas or diesel fuels the way the machine does.

## Better

- Long Chinese text breaks onto new lines instead of running off the edge.

## Fixed

- Generators and other power sources can be added to a plan again while you are signed in. They used to vanish at once with "An edit could not be saved", and edits to them were undone.
- Recipes with an empty slot, like the Bricked Blast Furnace's, no longer crash the game when you hover their add to plan button or bring them in from a plan.
- Fewer crashes on the board: hovering a card or port just as it changed, a machine count tip on a card that needs almost no machines, and going back to the planner from NEI's recipe page.
- The library no longer crashes on some plan icons.
- Clay Blocks, raw meat and the other things GregTech's recycling recipes take now wire into those recipes.
- The numpad types into rate and count boxes. An IME types Chinese into notes, task text and the other text boxes.
- Steam multiblocks and structure pictures show properly when the game is not in English.
